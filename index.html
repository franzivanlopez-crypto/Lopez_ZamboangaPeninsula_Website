About Zamboanga Peninsula

Zamboanga Peninsula (Region IX) is located in western Mindanao. It connects the provinces of Zamboanga del Norte, Zamboanga del Sur, and Zamboanga Sibugay with vibrant coastal cities and offshore islands.

Regional Note: Sulu became part of administrative Region IX in July 2025, and Isabela City is also part of Region IX. This guide focuses on the peninsula and nearby Zamboanga City islands.
